Al-Fajr

Add your first surah

Save a surah's MP3 in the Files app on your iPhone, then choose it here. The audio stays on your phone and works without internet. If it's Yasser Al-Dosari's Murattal of Al-Fajr, the app recognises it and sets up the verse cuts, Arabic text and meal for you.

Play
Order

Tap any verse to play from there. “Build up” plays verse 1, then 1–2, then 1–3, and so on. With text hidden, tapping a verse also reveals it for a few seconds. “Time to recite it back” pauses for as long as the verse took.

Fix verse timings

Choose a verse in the list, then move its start or end until it cuts cleanly. Changes save on this device.

    Choose the MP3 of one full surah. On iPhone, tap the file box, then “Choose File” and pick it from Files. The app finds the pauses between verses and splits it; you can fix any cut afterwards. The audio stays on this device.
